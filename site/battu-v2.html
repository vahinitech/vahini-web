📜 Why “Battu”?

A name with a thousand-year story.

Battu honours Nannaya Bhattaraka, the Adi Kavi, the first poet of Telugu. Around 1025 CE he began the Andhra Mahabharatam, bringing a great epic to everyday readers in their own language, and is credited with the first Telugu grammar.

Our classroom pen carries that same spirit: helping every child write, and love writing, in their own hand.
