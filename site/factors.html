The big picture

Four simple groups make up the score.

Every factor belongs to one of four groups. Each group counts for a share of the final score, shown beside its name. You don't need to know all 20 to start, the report always tells you the few that matter most for you.

Group 1

Structure

How each letter is shaped and controlled. 30% of score
1Letter Formation

Are your letters shaped correctly and consistently?

Good: each letter looks the way it should, every time you write it.

2Stroke Order

Do you build each letter with the same strokes in the same order?

Good: a steady, repeatable way of forming letters. Best with the pen

3Loop Closure

Do round letters like a, o, e and g close up neatly?

Good: closed loops, so an ‘a’ never reads like a ‘u’.

4Line Quality

Are your strokes smooth, or shaky and wobbly?

Good: clean, confident lines instead of jitter.

5Size Consistency

Are your letters all a similar size?

Good: even height, not some big and some tiny.

6Ascenders & Descenders

Do tall letters (b, h, l) and tails (g, y, p) reach the right length?

Good: talls and tails are balanced, not stunted or overgrown.

Group 2

Spacing & layout

How the writing sits on the page. 30% of score
7Baseline Alignment

Do your words sit on the line, or drift up and down?

Good: letters rest on an even invisible line.

8Word Spacing

Are the gaps between words even, not too tight or too wide?

Good: about one letter's width between words.

9Letter Spacing

Are the letters inside a word evenly spaced?

Good: letters neither cramped together nor spread apart.

10Margin Discipline

Do your lines start at a tidy, even left edge?

Good: a straight left margin down the whole page.

11Line Straightness

Do your lines run straight across, or slope off?

Good: level lines, even on blank, unruled paper.

12Vertical Alignment

Do your letters stand upright in a consistent way?

Good: uprights all lean the same, not in random directions.

Group 3

Movement & flow

How you move while writing, the pen measures this live. 20% of score
13Speed Consistency

Do you write at a steady pace?

Good: an even rhythm, not rushing then crawling. Best with the pen

14Pressure Consistency

Do you press with an even, relaxed force?

Good: steady pressure, not pressing hard and tiring out. Best with the pen

15Stroke Continuity

Do you join your strokes, or stop and start a lot?

Good: smooth, connected movement through a word. Best with the pen

16Pen Lifts

How often do you lift the pen in the middle of a word?

Good: few unnecessary lifts, the hand keeps flowing. Best with the pen

Group 4

Style & readability

How easy and how tidy the writing is to read. 20% of score
17Slant Consistency

Does your writing lean at one steady angle?

Good: a consistent slant, upright or italic, but the same throughout.

18Legibility

How easy is your writing to read at a glance?

Good: a stranger can read it without effort.

19Character Distinction

Are look-alike letters easy to tell apart?

Good: a/o, n/h and r/v are each unmistakable.

20Overall Neatness

How tidy does the whole page look, all together?

Good: even size, even spacing and straight lines add up to a clean page.

Four of the factors (movement & flow) measure how you move as you write. A photo gives a careful estimate; the Vahini sensor pen measures them live for the most accurate score.
