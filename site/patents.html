What it protects

The moat under the platform.

The patent covers the method that makes everything else possible, sensing the motion of writing from the pen itself, rather than imaging the page. That's why a camera company can't simply copy it.

Dual-IMU sensing

Two inertial units plus supporting sensors capture force, tilt, velocity and pen-lifts as the hand moves.

On ordinary paper

No dot-pattern, no tablet, no special notebook. The signal comes from the pen, the page is just paper.

Motion-to-meaning method

The path from raw inertial signal to interpretable handwriting metrics, the basis of every Vahini solution.
