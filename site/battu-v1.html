📜 Why “Battu”?

Named for the first poet of Telugu.

Battu honours Nannaya Bhattaraka, remembered as the Adi Kavi, the very first poet of the Telugu language, who wrote around the 11th century.

When King Rajaraja Narendra wanted the great Mahabharata to reach everyone in their own language, he turned to Nannaya. Around 1025 CE he began the Andhra Mahabharatam, and is even credited with the first book of Telugu grammar, opening the written word to a whole people.

A thousand years on, our little pen carries that same wish: to help a new generation fall in love with writing, by hand, in their own language.
