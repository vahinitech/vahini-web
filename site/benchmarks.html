Writing quality

What the pen actually captures

Six things about your writing, in plain words. Together they are the raw material for every report the analyser gives you.

Pressure Control

How firmly the tip meets the paper, and how steadily you hold that pressure through a word.

Pen Control

The angle you hold the pen at and how well you keep it, a quiet signature of grip and comfort.

Writing Speed

How fast the tip travels, stroke by stroke, and where you slow down or rush.

Writing Flow

The natural pace and continuity of your strokes: fluent and even, or hesitant and effortful.

Hand Stability

Steadiness of the hand while writing, smooth control versus shake and wobble.

Smoothness

How cleanly each stroke starts, turns and ends, without jerks or corrections.

Specifications

What is inside, in one table

SensingTwo independent motion sensors (one near the tip, one near the top), a compass, and a pressure sensor in the tip. The two motion sensors see every stroke from two points, which is what makes faithful reconstruction possible.
Sampling208 motion snapshots per second. Handwriting changes within milliseconds; this rate catches hesitations and shakes a photograph never can.
PaperAny paper, any notebook, any form. No special dot-pattern, no tablet, no screen.
ConnectionBluetooth Low Energy to your phone or laptop, live while you write. USB-C for charging and wired transfer.
Offline memoryThe pen keeps writing when nothing is connected: up to around 19 hours of pure writing time stored on the pen itself, which covers several weeks of normal daily notes. Sync whenever you like; nothing is lost in between.
IndicatorA single colour light tells you everything: recording, battery, charging, pairing, or a warning. One meaning per colour, documented in the manual.
ScriptsMotion capture is script-neutral by design. The analyser currently reads five Indic scripts, with more in training.
Size & weightA normal pen in the hand. Final production dimensions and weight will be published here at launch; shrinking both without giving up accuracy is an explicit design goal.

Battery & charging

Writes all day. Charges over lunch.

Continuous writingAround 15 hours of non-stop recording on one charge. Nobody writes 15 hours straight; that is the point.
A typical dayWith a couple of hours of writing a day, expect roughly four days between charges.
On the shelfA charged pen left in a drawer keeps its charge for weeks to months.
Full chargeAbout 2 hours over USB-C, from empty to full.
Forgot it on the charger?Safe. Charging stops automatically when the battery is full; leaving the pen plugged in overnight, or for a weekend, does not harm it. Charging protection is built in twice: once in the charging hardware and once in the pen's own software, and each layer works even if the other fails.
Low batteryThe pen warns you early, saves everything, and shuts down cleanly before the battery can be damaged. Your notes are never the price of an empty battery.

Everyday life

Built for real hands, including small ones

A pen lives a rough life. It gets dropped, shaken, chewed, forgotten in bags, and borrowed by children. We test for that life deliberately: drops from desk height are detected in flight, a pen rattling in a backpack does not wake up and drain itself, and banging or shaking the pen does not corrupt what you wrote. If something does go wrong, the pen recovers on its own at the next start and keeps your notes intact.

Heat is handled the same way. Writing while charging on a warm day stays well inside safe limits; if the pen ever gets genuinely hot, it warns you first and only stops if it must.

How we measure, honestly. The numbers on this page come from our engineering bench and from a full simulation of the pen's electronics that we run on every code change, using the same power figures our hardware is designed to. Pre-production hardware confirms them; final retail units will be re-measured and this page updated at launch. Where a number is a target rather than a measurement, we say so.
