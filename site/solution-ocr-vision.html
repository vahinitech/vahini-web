Why we built it

A useful tool, not a product we sell.

Plenty of apps read printed text; few handle handwritten Indic script, because vattus, guninthas and joined modifiers make it genuinely hard and labelled data is scarce. That’s exactly what our pen and dataset are good at, so we put a small reader online for anyone to use, free. It earns its keep two ways: it’s genuinely helpful, and it brings people who need handwriting tech to Vahini. We are not selling an OCR model; the real products are the pen, the analyser and the dataset.
