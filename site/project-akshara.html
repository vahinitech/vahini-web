Why it doesn't exist yet

A photo keeps the shape.
Motion keeps the story.

A photograph shows what a letter ended up looking like. The recording shows the hand slow before a tricky loop, press harder on a retry, hesitate at the same joint twice. That second layer, real hands, real paper, in the scripts research forgot, is what Akshara collects.

A marathon, not a sprint

Years are the moat money can't buy.

Every sample is written by a real hand, page after page, school after school. It can't be scraped, rushed or bought. Granted hardware IP (Patent No. 584433), a head start measured in years, and school trust earned consent-first, three layers, each protecting the others.

How collection works

Simple on the surface.
Careful underneath.

  • Schools write, as they already do. Ordinary paper, normal class time.
  • Consent first, always. Opt-in, guardian sign-off, withdrawable any time.
  • Motion recorded, identity separated. Pressure and pace join the corpus; names don't.
  • Contributors benefit immediately. Coaching reports from day one, quality-checked as it grows.

Responsible by design

Built the slow way, because it's the right way

Children are involved, so we set the bar ourselves before anyone asked: plain-language consent, guardian sign-off for minors, motion separated from identity, and the right to withdraw at any time. A dataset built with schools only works if schools can trust it for years.
